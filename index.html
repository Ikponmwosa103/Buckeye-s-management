A LEGACY OF CRAFTSMANSHIP

Decades of experience.
A commitment to precision.

Good work stands the test of time. So do the relationships behind it.

Buckeye State Welding & Fabricating began at its current machine shop location in 1957 and was incorporated in 1975. Across three generations, the company has continued to support customers with machining, fabrication and assembly expertise.

From job-shop repair and maintenance machining to structural steel fabrication, the team focuses on understanding each project and delivering quality workmanship.
