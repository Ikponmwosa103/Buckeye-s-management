A LEGACY OF CRAFTSMANSHIP

Decades of experience.
A commitment to precision.

Good work stands the test of time. So do the relationships behind it.

Buckeye State Welding & Fabricating began at its current machine shop location in 1957 and was incorporated in 1975. Across three generations, the company has continued to support customers with machining, fabrication and assembly expertise.

From job-shop repair and maintenance machining to structural steel fabrication, the team focuses on understanding each project and delivering quality workmanship.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Answers that help
projects move faster.

What types of work does Buckeye handle?

Machining (job-shop repair and maintenance of light and heavy steel and other materials), welding and structural fabrication, flame and plasma cutting, and assembly/disassembly of industrial equipment such as gearboxes, rolls and conveyor systems.

Are the welders certified?

Yes. The company states that its welders are D1.1 certified. Available processes include Stick, TIG, MIG and oxygen-acetylene.

Where are the facilities located?

Two locations in Elyria, Ohio: the Machine Shop at 131 Buckeye Street and the Fabrication Shop at 175 Woodford Avenue. Contact the appropriate facility for the work you need.

Can Buckeye support urgent or outage-related work?

Customer feedback highlights responsiveness in emergency situations and the ability to support tight outage schedules. Daily truck delivery further supports time-sensitive projects.

How do I request a quote?

Use the quote form on this page or call the Machine Shop (440.322.0319) or Fabrication Shop (440.323.6831) directly. Providing drawings, material requirements and target dates helps the team respond accurately.
